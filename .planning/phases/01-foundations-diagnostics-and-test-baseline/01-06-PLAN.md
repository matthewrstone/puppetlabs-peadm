<objective>
Replace the fixed readiness wait with an overridable deadline end to end (task, function, plans) and make a timed-out wait explain itself (FND-07; FND-01 for the service-wrapping wait task, D-04).

Purpose: slow nodes stop failing on a hard-coded 60 s, and when a wait does expire the operator sees what was awaited and why it was not ready, including the CA readiness wait that `add_replica` will use once its endpoint is confirmed on a real primary.
Output: task with `timeout`/`interval`, function with `timeout`, `service_ready_timeout` on add_replica, upgrade and convert, specs, ledger rows P1-08 and P1-09, fix-log entry.

Naming decision (Claude's discretion for FND-07, recorded here so later plans reuse it): task params `timeout` and `interval` (seconds); function argument `$timeout`; public plan parameter `Integer[1] $service_ready_timeout = 60`. Plans 01-10 (install) and 01-11 (restore) add the same plan parameter. "The CA timeout" in FND-07 is read as the `ca` service readiness wait (RESEARCH assumption A4); GJira-11 (PE core's own CA query timeouts inside enable) is Related, not fixed. Checker warning W5: the `/status/v1/simple/ca` endpoint has not been seen on a real PE primary, so this plan does NOT wire a `ca` wait into add_replica; plan 01-13's node check probes the endpoint on 2025.12.1 and 2026.0.1, and plan 01-14 adds the add_replica call only if it answered.
</objective>
